🏠 Parent Activity Guide

Use these simple steps to support your child's ABC learning.

🐱 1. Read Together

Read the ABC Story and encourage your child to say letters A–E aloud.

🔎 2. Explore

Let your child tap or click the letters, pictures, and answers.

💬 3. Ask & Encourage

Ask your child to name the letters and pictures. Praise their effort.

⭐ 4. Practice Again

Repeat activities to strengthen letter recognition and ABC order.
